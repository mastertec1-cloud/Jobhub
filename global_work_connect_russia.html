Protect yourself during international recruitment

This page does not guarantee a job, visa, salary, or travel approval. Always verify every offer and the recruiter independently.

  • Do not pay unexpected fees or send money until you have independently verified the employer and the legal basis for any charge.
  • Never surrender your passport to an unverified person or sign a contract you do not understand.
  • Confirm current work authorization requirements with official Russian authorities or your country's embassy/consulate.
